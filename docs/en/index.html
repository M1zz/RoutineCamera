FAQ

Where are my photos stored?

By default, only on your device. If you turn on saving to the Photos app in Settings, they're also saved to Sekki albums in Photos. If you turn on sharing, they're uploaded so the friends you add can see them.

How do I add friends?

On the Friends screen, share your six-character friend code and enter your friend's code. You can also paste several codes at once, separated by commas.

How do I cheer on a friend?

Open a friend's meal and leave an emoji or a short comment. If they haven't logged a meal yet, tap the empty slot to send a gentle nudge.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Friends, then Account Settings, then Delete Account. Your account and the meal data stored on the server are deleted and can't be recovered. If anything goes wrong, just email us.

I'm not getting notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for Sekki in iOS Settings, then Notifications, and that your device is signed in to iCloud. You can change your meal reminder times in the app's Settings.
